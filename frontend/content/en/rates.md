All bookings and payments are made on Airbnb. You cannot book or pay on this website.

## What makes the rate vary

- Day of the week (weekdays / Fridays, Saturdays and days before a holiday)
- Season (summer, autumn-leaves season, the New Year period, long holidays and so on)
- Local events, when you book, and the length of your stay

## What the rate includes

- **Room**: exclusive use of the guest room (5.2 tatami mats), a queen bed and a projector
- **Shared facilities**: the LDK (living, dining and kitchen), pantry, laundry room, bathroom, changing room and toilet (some shared with the host)
- **Amenities**: coffee and snacks in the pantry, a toothbrush set, toner and lotion
- **Parking**: free (1 car)
- **Wi-Fi**: free

## Conditions of stay

- **Capacity**: 1–2 guests (stays above capacity cannot be accepted)
- **Infants and young children**: the home does not adequately provide for the safety of infants and young children, so we ask guests to refrain from staying with children aged 5 or under
- **Check-in**: from 15:00
- **Check-out**: by 10:00
- **Smoking**: no smoking anywhere on the premises
- **Curfew and quiet hours**: no curfew; 22:00–05:00 is quiet time (no noise)

## Cancellation

Cancellations are made through Airbnb, and refunds follow Airbnb's cancellation policy.

- **Up to 5 days before the check-in date**: full refund
- **Less than 5 days before the check-in date**: partial refund under Airbnb's cancellation policy

For details, see Appended Table 2 of the [terms of accommodation](/en/terms).

Rates as of October 1, 2026. Rates may change without notice.
