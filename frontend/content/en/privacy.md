## What we collect

Guest registration collects what we need for the guest register: name, address, contact details, nationality and, for guests without an address in Japan, the passport number.

## Why we use it

To keep and manage the guest register, to contact you about your stay, and to meet legal obligations.

## How we protect it

Passport numbers are stored encrypted, and only the last 4 characters are ever shown or exported. Only hosts who have been granted access can use the admin console.
