## Article 1 (Scope)

**1** Accommodation contracts and related contracts concluded between Saku no Ie (the "Facility") and guests are governed by these terms. Matters not provided for in these terms are governed by laws and regulations (meaning laws and regulations, and anything based on them; the same applies below) or by generally established custom.

**2** If the Facility agrees to a special arrangement to the extent that it does not violate laws and regulations or custom, that special arrangement takes precedence, notwithstanding the preceding paragraph.

**3** The Facility is a host-occupied simple lodging (家主居住型 簡易宿所) in which the host (the operator of the Facility) lives in the same building. The guest room is a room for the guests' exclusive use, but some facilities, such as the bathroom, washroom and laundry room, are shared with the host.

**4** For accommodation contracts concluded through an accommodation booking site such as Airbnb (a "booking site"), if the booking site's terms (including its cancellation policy and payment conditions) differ from these terms, the booking site's terms take precedence.

## Article 2 (Application for an accommodation contract)

**1** A person who wishes to apply to the Facility for an accommodation contract is asked to tell the Facility the following:

1. The guests' names and the number of guests
1. The dates of stay and the expected time of arrival
1. The accommodation charge (in principle, the basic accommodation charge in Appended Table 1)
1. Any other matters the Facility considers necessary

**2** If a guest, during the stay, asks to continue the stay beyond the dates of stay in item 2 of the preceding paragraph, the Facility treats the request as a new application for an accommodation contract made at the time of the request.

## Article 3 (Conclusion of the accommodation contract, etc.)

**1** An accommodation contract is concluded when the Facility accepts the application under the preceding article. This does not apply if the Facility proves that it did not accept it.

**2** For applications through a booking site, the accommodation contract is concluded when the booking is confirmed on the booking site, and the accommodation charge is paid by the method that the booking site specifies.

**3** For direct applications not made through a booking site, the guest is asked to pay, by the date the Facility specifies, a deposit set by the Facility of up to the basic accommodation charge for the period of stay (3 days if the stay exceeds 3 days) once the accommodation contract is concluded.

**4** The deposit is first applied to the accommodation charge the guest finally has to pay. If a situation arises to which Article 6 or Article 18 applies, it is applied to the cancellation charge and then to compensation for damages, in that order. Any remainder is returned when the charges are paid under Article 12.

**5** If the deposit under paragraph 3 is not paid by the date the Facility specified, the accommodation contract loses its effect. This applies only if the Facility told the guest so when it specified the payment date of the deposit.

## Article 4 (Special arrangement not requiring a deposit)

**1** Notwithstanding paragraph 3 of the preceding article, the Facility may agree, after the contract is concluded, to a special arrangement under which the deposit in that paragraph is not required.

**2** If, when accepting an application for an accommodation contract, the Facility did not ask for the deposit under paragraph 3 of the preceding article, or did not specify a payment date for it, the Facility is treated as having agreed to the special arrangement in the preceding paragraph.

## Article 4-2 (Request for cooperation with infection-prevention measures at the Facility)

The Facility may ask a person who wishes to stay for the cooperation provided for in Article 4-2, paragraph 1 of the Inns and Hotels Act (Act No. 138 of 1948).

## Article 5 (Refusal to conclude an accommodation contract)

**1** The Facility may decline to conclude an accommodation contract in the following cases. This paragraph does not mean that the Facility may refuse a stay in cases other than those listed in Article 5 of the Inns and Hotels Act.

1. The application is not made in accordance with these terms.
1. No room is available because the Facility is fully booked.
1. The person who wishes to stay is found likely to act, in connection with the stay, against the provisions of laws and regulations, public order or good morals.
1. The person who wishes to stay is found to fall under any of (a) to (c) below.
   - (a) An organized crime group as defined in Article 2, item 2 of the Act on Prevention of Unjust Acts by Organized Crime Group Members (Act No. 77 of 1991) (an "organized crime group"), an organized crime group member as defined in Article 2, item 6 of that Act (an "organized crime group member"), a quasi-member of an organized crime group, a person associated with an organized crime group, or any other anti-social force
   - (b) A corporation or other organization whose business activities are controlled by an organized crime group or an organized crime group member
   - (c) A corporation with an organized crime group member among its officers
1. The person who wishes to stay has behaved in a way that causes significant nuisance to other guests or neighbors.
1. The person who wishes to stay is a patient, etc. of a specified infectious disease as provided for in Article 4-2, paragraph 1, item 2 of the Inns and Hotels Act (a "patient, etc. of a specified infectious disease").
1. A violent demand is made in connection with the stay, or a burden beyond a reasonable range is demanded (except where the person who wishes to stay requests the removal of social barriers under Article 7, paragraph 2 or Article 8, paragraph 2 of the Act for Eliminating Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities (Act No. 65 of 2013; the "Act for Eliminating Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities")).
1. The person who wishes to stay has repeatedly made demands of the Facility that are specified in Article 5-6 of the Ordinance for Enforcement of the Inns and Hotels Act as demands whose fulfillment is excessively burdensome and likely to significantly hinder accommodation services for other guests.
1. The Facility cannot accommodate the person because of a natural disaster, a breakdown of facilities or other unavoidable reasons.
1. The case falls under Article 9 of the Nagano Prefecture Ordinance for Enforcement of the Inns and Hotels Act.

**2** An application for more guests than the room's capacity (2 people) is treated as a case in which no room is available under item 2 of the preceding paragraph.

## Article 5-2 (Explanation of a refusal to conclude an accommodation contract)

If the Facility declines to conclude an accommodation contract under the preceding article, the person who wishes to stay may ask the Facility to explain the reason.

## Article 5-3 (Request regarding stays with infants and young children)

The guest room of the Facility is a room in an ordinary home, and its facilities do not adequately provide for the safety of infants and young children. We therefore ask guests to refrain from staying with children aged 5 or under. This article does not establish a ground for refusing to conclude an accommodation contract.

## Article 6 (Guests' right to cancel the contract)

**1** A guest may cancel the accommodation contract by notifying the Facility.

**2** If a guest cancels all or part of the accommodation contract for reasons attributable to the guest, the Facility charges a cancellation fee as set out in Appended Table 2 (except where the Facility specified a payment date for the deposit under Article 3, paragraph 3 and requested payment, and the guest cancelled the contract before paying it). However, if the Facility agreed to the special arrangement in Article 4, paragraph 1, this applies only if the Facility informed the guest, when agreeing to that arrangement, of the guest's obligation to pay a cancellation fee on cancelling the accommodation contract.

**3** If a guest does not arrive on the day of stay without contacting the Facility, the handling and any refund follow the Airbnb cancellation policy that applies to the booking.

## Article 7 (The Facility's right to cancel the contract)

**1** The Facility may cancel the accommodation contract in the following cases. This paragraph does not mean that the Facility may refuse a stay in cases other than those listed in Article 5 of the Inns and Hotels Act.

1. The guest is found likely to act, or to have acted, in connection with the stay, against the provisions of laws and regulations, public order or good morals.
1. The guest is found to fall under any of (a) to (c) below.
   - (a) An organized crime group, an organized crime group member, a quasi-member of an organized crime group, a person associated with an organized crime group, or any other anti-social force
   - (b) A corporation or other organization whose business activities are controlled by an organized crime group or an organized crime group member
   - (c) A corporation with an organized crime group member among its officers
1. The guest has behaved in a way that causes significant nuisance to other guests or neighbors.
1. The guest is a patient, etc. of a specified infectious disease.
1. A violent demand is made in connection with the stay, or a burden beyond a reasonable range is demanded (except where the guest requests the removal of social barriers under Article 7, paragraph 2 or Article 8, paragraph 2 of the Act for Eliminating Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities).
1. The guest has repeatedly made demands of the Facility that are specified in Article 5-6 of the Ordinance for Enforcement of the Inns and Hotels Act as demands whose fulfillment is excessively burdensome and likely to significantly hinder accommodation services for other guests.
1. The Facility cannot accommodate the guest for reasons caused by force majeure, such as a natural disaster.
1. The case falls under Article 9 of the Nagano Prefecture Ordinance for Enforcement of the Inns and Hotels Act.
1. The guest does not comply with prohibitions in the Facility's house rules (limited to those necessary for fire prevention), such as smoking on the premises or tampering with fire-fighting equipment.

**2** If the Facility cancels the accommodation contract under the preceding paragraph, it does not charge for accommodation services and the like that the guest has not yet received.

## Article 7-2 (Explanation of a cancellation of the accommodation contract)

If the Facility cancels the accommodation contract under the preceding article, the guest may ask the Facility to explain the reason.

## Article 8 (Registration of guests)

**1** On the day of stay, guests are asked to register the following at the Facility:

1. The guest's name, address and contact details
1. For foreign nationals without an address in Japan, their nationality and passport number (we ask for your cooperation in presenting your passport and in letting us keep a copy of it)
1. Any other matters the Facility considers necessary

**2** If a guest intends to pay the charges under Article 12 by a method that can substitute for cash, such as a credit card, the guest is asked to present it in advance at the registration in the preceding paragraph (except where payment has already been made on a booking site).

## Article 9 (Hours of room use)

**1** Guests may use the Facility's room from 3:00 p.m. until 10:00 a.m. the next morning. For consecutive nights, the room may be used all day, except on the day of arrival and the day of departure.

**2** Notwithstanding the preceding paragraph, the Facility may allow use of the room outside the hours in that paragraph. In that case, an additional charge, which will be explained separately, may apply.

## Article 10 (Compliance with house rules)

**1** Within the Facility, guests are asked to follow the house rules that the Facility has set and posted in the Facility or explained in advance.

**2** The house rules in the preceding paragraph include the following:

1. Guests may enter the guest room, the LDK (living, dining and kitchen), the pantry, the laundry room, the toilet, the changing room and the bathroom, and must not enter the master bedroom or the study.
1. Smoking is prohibited everywhere on the premises (including e-cigarettes and heated tobacco products).
1. Do not hold parties, play loud music or do anything else that disturbs the neighbors. In particular, 10:00 p.m. to 5:00 a.m. is quiet time, and noisy activities are prohibited.
1. Do not let anyone other than the registered guests into the Facility.

## Article 11 (Service hours)

**1** The Facility's main service hours are as follows:

1. Check-in: from 3:00 p.m. (please let us know your expected time of arrival in advance)
1. Check-out: by 10:00 a.m.
1. Curfew: none (10:00 p.m. to 5:00 a.m. is quiet time; please come and go quietly at night)
1. Inquiries: accepted at any time through the booking site's messaging function or the contact details the Facility provides (Japanese and English)

**2** The hours in the preceding paragraph may be changed temporarily when necessary and unavoidable. In that case, we will inform you by an appropriate method.

## Article 12 (Payment of charges)

**1** The breakdown of the accommodation charges and other charges the guest has to pay is as set out in Appended Table 1.

**2** The accommodation charges and other charges in the preceding paragraph are paid by the method that the booking site specifies for bookings through a booking site, and otherwise in cash or by a substitute method accepted by the Facility, such as a credit card, on the guest's arrival or when the Facility requests payment.

**3** The accommodation charge is payable even if the guest voluntarily does not stay after the Facility has provided the room and it has become available for use.

## Article 13 (Liability of the Facility)

If the Facility causes damage to a guest in performing, or by failing to perform, the accommodation contract and related contracts, it compensates for the damage. This does not apply if the damage is not due to reasons attributable to the Facility.

## Article 14 (Handling when the contracted room cannot be provided)

**1** If the Facility cannot provide the contracted room, it will, with the guest's consent, arrange other accommodation on conditions as close to the same as possible.

**2** Notwithstanding the preceding paragraph, if the Facility cannot arrange other accommodation, it pays the guest compensation equivalent to the cancellation fee, and this compensation is applied to the amount of damages. However, if the inability to provide the room is not due to reasons attributable to the Facility, no compensation is paid.

## Article 15 (Handling of belongings, etc.)

**1** The Facility does not take custody of guests' belongings, cash or valuables. Guests are responsible for managing the belongings, cash and valuables they bring into the Facility.

**2** If the belongings, cash or valuables in the preceding paragraph are lost, damaged or otherwise harmed through the intent or negligence of the Facility, the Facility compensates for the damage.

## Article 16 (Custody of guests' baggage or belongings)

**1** If a guest's baggage arrives at the Facility before the stay, the Facility keeps it responsibly only if it agreed to do so before the baggage arrived, and hands it over when the guest checks in.

**2** If a guest's baggage or belongings are left behind at the Facility after check-out and the owner is identified, the Facility contacts the owner and asks for instructions. If the owner gives no instructions or cannot be identified, the Facility keeps the items for 7 days including the day they were found, and then hands them over to the nearest police station. Items not suitable for keeping, such as food and drink, may be disposed of at the Facility's discretion.

**3** The Facility's liability for keeping a guest's baggage or belongings in the cases of the preceding two paragraphs follows paragraph 2 of the preceding article.

## Article 17 (Liability for parking)

When a guest uses the Facility's parking space, the Facility only lends the space, whether or not the vehicle's keys are left with it, and does not take responsibility for managing the vehicle. However, if the Facility causes damage through its intent or negligence in managing the parking space, it is liable for compensation.

## Article 18 (Liability of guests)

If the Facility suffers damage through the intent or negligence of a guest, that guest is asked to compensate the Facility for the damage.
